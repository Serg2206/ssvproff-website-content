# Welcome to SSVproff

## Surgery, Science, and Artificial Intelligence in Medicine

A professional platform combining clinical practice, scientific research, and innovative technologies in surgery and oncology.

### About the Platform

SSVproff is a space for sharing knowledge and experience in:

- **Surgery and Surgical Oncology** — modern treatment methods, clinical cases, surgical techniques
- **Cryotherapy** — application of low temperatures in oncology treatment
- **Artificial Intelligence in Medicine** — RAG systems, complication prediction, medical data analysis
- **Digital Healthcare** — technology integration in clinical practice

### What You'll Find Here

- **Scientific Publications** — articles, research, clinical observations
- **Blog** — thoughts on medicine, technology, and science
- **AI Lab** — interactive demonstrations of AI applications in medicine
- **Educational Materials** — guides, reviews, practical recommendations

### Mission

To combine clinical expertise with modern technologies to improve the quality of medical care and advance scientific knowledge.
